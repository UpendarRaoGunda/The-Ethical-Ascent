The integrity compass

Six questions before an irreversible decision.

  1. 01
    Evidence

    What supports the claim? What would falsify it?

  2. 02
    Uncertainty

    What do we not know, and have we said it clearly?

  3. 03
    Reversibility

    If we are wrong, can ecological damage be undone?

  4. 04
    Interdependence

    What hidden food webs and relationships are affected?

  5. 05
    Transparency

    Who benefits, who bears risk, and are conflicts visible?

  6. 06
    Future life

    Does this preserve options for species and generations not in the room?

The living-world integrity charter

Science is not only a way of knowing. It is a way of being answerable to reality.

01I will prefer evidence over ego.

02I will state uncertainty instead of hiding it.

03I will distinguish what is from what I merely wish were true.

04I will treat irreversible ecological harm with special caution.

05I will remember that humans are participants in nature, not spectators above it.

06I will judge progress partly by what life remains possible after us.
