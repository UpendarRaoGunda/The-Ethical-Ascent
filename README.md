# The Ethical Ascent

**Science × Integrity × Life**

An interactive public-learning website about scientific evidence, intellectual integrity, biodiversity, and humanity's responsibility toward the living world.

[![Deploy to Render](https://render.com/images/deploy-to-render-button.svg)](https://render.com/deploy?repo=https://github.com/UpendarRaoGunda/The-Ethical-Ascent)

The project begins with Jacob Bronowski's argument in *The Ascent of Man* that knowledge carries responsibility for our integrity as ethical creatures, then expands the conversation through Rachel Carson, Jane Goodall, Aldo Leopold, Charles Darwin, Carl Sagan, E. O. Wilson, Sylvia Earle, Wangari Maathai, Barry Commoner, J. Howard Moore, David Attenborough, and others.

## Experiences

- Hall of Ideas — searchable thinkers, books, quotations, and editorial syntheses
- Reading Constellation — books connecting evolution, ecology, evidence, and ethics
- Species Lens — the five major direct biodiversity drivers highlighted by IPBES
- Integrity Lab — interactive conservation dilemmas
- Integrity Compass — evidence, uncertainty, reversibility, interdependence, transparency, and future-life lenses
- Living-World Integrity Charter — a local-only personal commitment
- Source Discipline — source links and clear separation of direct quotation from editorial summary

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or backend is required.

## Render

This repository includes `render.yaml` for a Render static-site deployment. Use the **Deploy to Render** button above to create the static site from the Blueprint, or connect this repository from the Render dashboard.

## Content integrity

Direct quotations are identified as quotations. Editorial paraphrases/syntheses are labeled. Interactive decision scores are teaching heuristics, not scientific or regulatory risk calculators.
